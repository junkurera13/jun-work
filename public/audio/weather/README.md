# Weather ambience credits

These CC0 recordings are used as edited, crossfaded 38-second loops. They are downloaded from BigSoundBank / La Sonothèque; the original recordings and license information are linked below.

- Snow (winter wind): [Wind](https://bigsoundbank.com/wind-s0595.html), Joseph Sardin.
- Rain: [Rain on concrete](https://bigsoundbank.com/rain-on-concrete-s1289.html), Joseph Sardin.
- Breeze: [Wind in Tall Grass](https://bigsoundbank.com/wind-in-tall-grass-s0908.html), Joseph Sardin.
- Sunshine: [Birds in the City #1](https://bigsoundbank.com/birds-in-the-city-1-s3575.html), Joseph Sardin.
- Heatwave: [Cicadas](https://bigsoundbank.com/cicadas-s3002.html), Clarisse.

The originals are released under CC0. The files here are trimmed, loop-crossfaded, loudness-matched, and encoded as MP3.
