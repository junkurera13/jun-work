Pluck

A video player you can play.

I wanted scrubbing through a video to feel like something you touch, so I turned the timeline into a guitar string. Grab it, pull, let go, and it actually twangs. Each chapter is a fret with its own note, so dragging across the video plays a little tune. The volume is a second string that gets tighter as you turn it up.

The sound is made in the browser, nothing is downloaded. All the normal Video.js controls are still underneath, so it works fine with a keyboard and on phones.
